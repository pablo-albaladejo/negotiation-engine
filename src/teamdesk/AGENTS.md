# src/teamdesk/ — team offers desk

Counteroffers to the offers **other teams make us** (to-me) that El Rastro rejects for margin (`below-margin`). Sales only and duplicates only: the team asks for a card of ours for cash and we answer with a targeted offer (`to` = that team, same venue) of that card if we have a spare copy; we never buy. **The last free copy is never offered** (Pablo's decision, 3 Oct: complete pages are not sold), neither of the requested card nor of another. Only the structure is read, never their text. Used by `pnpm bazaar:play`; POSTs go out only live and with `--team-desk` (opt-in), and otherwise they are «would» lines.

## Files

- **`counter.ts`** — pure: `proposeTeamDesk`. It does not counteroffer a team whose points room is < `MIN_ROOM` (`room` of `DeskInput`, from [`src/markets/room.ts`](../markets/AGENTS.md): Payday's cap is per counterparty). **CHA lane (P7, coordinator OK, 4 Oct):** the only exception to «never the last copy». A team offer rejected for `last-free-copy` is counteroffered at its same price if three conditions hold: a dealer resells it to us at ≤ our value (`rebuyable`, from `dealerBuyQuotes` in `src/forex/ledger.ts`, 600-tick window and no Pícaros); the capped gain, min(price − commission − value, team room), is ≥ `laneMinGain` (20); and the price stays ≥ value + 20. That counteroffer never lowers its price. **It stops with the page complete** (coordinator OK, 4 Oct, with CHA 10/10): if we have all the set's page cards, the last copy does not go out and the open counteroffer for that card is cancelled. The album planner rebuys it from the dealer afterwards (the «dealers» session). The coordinator fills it in [`src/coordinator/main.ts`](../coordinator/AGENTS.md). The figure comes from the code: the floor (`deskFloor`) is the copy's `your_value` plus a margin (the greater of 2 P and 10 % of the price) and the venue's commission; the anchor is floor × 1.14. It is lowered with `slowReprice` every 6 ticks while the team's offer stays open, always through `enforceGuardrails` (monotonic, never below the floor). It also offers a duplicate that this team asks for (`RivalSignals.demand`). It never counteroffers a hidden or collection card (`isKeepsake`, Pablo's rule: hidden ones are not sold), nor a legendary, an epic, one of unknown rarity or a copy with `your_value` ≤ 0 (egg gift like LAT-13: no floor given by the code, it needs a price from Pablo). It does not use busy assets (other offers of ours, what El Rastro publishes, locked or reserved). Maximum 3 open; it never operates in v01, v02, v07 or v14. It only lowers or cancels those it published itself (`deskOfferIds`); the targeted ones from rival-page and rival-swap are not its own.
- **`desk.ts`** — `executeTeamDesk`: cancellations first, then POSTs. Right before publishing it rereads `busyAssets` and the server's value (`/api/me/value`), and raises the floor if that value rose. Record in `results/bazaar-live/<date>/team-desk.jsonl` (`incoming`, `counter`, `step`, `cancel` and `outcome` with the neg Δ), which the viewer's «Team desk» card reads. On startup, `loadDeskLedger` seeds what was sent today.
