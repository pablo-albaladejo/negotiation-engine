# src/duels/ — Duels

1-on-1 duels between teams (`GET /api/duels`, one message per side per tick; duel acceptances have their own limit and do not spend the team's quota, according to the official duels PDF; the pie shrinks with each round). The figure comes from the engine, the text is a template with those same figures and `your_limit` is never revealed; from the rival only the structured offer is read (`normalizeRivalOffer`), never their text. Entry point: `main.ts` (`pnpm bazaar:duels`).

## Files

- **`schemas.ts`** — Tolerant Zod for `/api/duels` (includes `messages[]` and `your_offer`) and `/api/schedule`, `duelMessageBody` (with days: `price` and `days` also inside `offer`, like the SDK) and `duelsApi`, an adapter over `BazaarClient` without touching `../shared/client.ts`. `rivalOfferFrom` reads the rival's current offer from `rival_offer` or, if missing, from the price of their last message (`from` field ≠ "you"; never their text, stale offer fallback). `ourOfferFrom` reads our last offer from `your_offer`.
- **`duels.ts`** — `decideDuel`, pure: own surplus in P (seller price − limit, buyer limit − price, plus `daysValueFrom(your_days_weight)`); opening `anchorMargin` (0.35: seller limit × 1.35, buyer limit ÷ 1.35), the engine's `concession` curve with β 2 down to a floor (`floorShare` 0.3 of the reference surplus, `referenceMargin` 0.5) over `maxRounds` (3) messages; the engine's `decideAcceptance` (AC_next; last tick: anything that leaves ≥ `minSurplus`); `enforceGuardrails` on price (never crosses the limit) and on surplus (monotonicity). It never concedes without a new rival offer since ours: if the rival stays silent, it waits without a message, however many ticks pass (we do not "negotiate against ourselves"). Exception only in the last `endgameTicks`: if the rival has ever offered, it moves toward a deal by splitting the difference (without going below the `floorShare` floor or crossing `your_limit`); if they never offered, the current offer is held. With days: logrolling in `offerForSurplus` (the day that maximizes our value minus what we estimate the rival loses by moving away from their day, `estimateRivalDaysWeight`; ties, their day) and the price compensates to keep the target surplus. `textMatchesOffer` requires that the only figures in the text are price and days. Templates (`DUEL_TEMPLATES`) always polite (*please* / *thank*) and free of phrases from the blacklist of `src/dealers/negotiation/messages.ts`.
- **`agent.ts`** — `DuelsAgent`, propose method (decision for each live duel, GET only) and execute method (acceptances in the given order, one per duel; messages only for the chosen duels); `DuelsAgent.step` = both with their own selection: one step per tick, all acceptances of that tick (one per duel), server errors without breaking the loop; message authors from `from` field; `ticksLeft` (tick, ISO or epoch) and `formatNextDuels` for the dry-run. Per-duel memory (our offers and the rival's) in the process and, with `stateFile`, also on disk (`saveDuelsMemory`/`loadDuelsMemory`, atomic rename, by default `results/bazaar-live/<date>/duels-state.json` via `defaultDuelsStateFile`) so a restart does not reopen with a new anchor or repeat the first message. If there is no memory for a duel (restart without a file), `stateOf` seeds our last offer from `your_offer` and reads the rival's with `rivalOfferFrom`.
- **`main.ts`** — `pnpm bazaar:duels --dry-run --once` (GET only: prints what it would do or "no duels scheduled now" with the next session). Flags: `--restart-check` (see v3), `--max-rounds`, `--beta`, `--anchor-margin`, `--floor-share`, `--assumed-days-weight`, `--state-file` (live, by default `results/bazaar-live/<date>/duels-state.json`; none in `--dry-run`).

## Duels v2 (3 Oct)

Measured over the 27 practice duels: 17 deals, 10 with no deal, average 15.4 P per duel. The result of a duel is our surplus × (1 − `decay_per_round`)^rounds. 8 of the 10 with no deal had a silent rival (never offered; 2 silent rivals did accept our opening) and the remaining 2, a single offer outside our limit. The 6–7 round haggles (54, 243) ended at 5–6 P because of decay. Changes: `maxRounds` 3 (was 4); accept early (`accept-share`: their offer leaves ≥ `acceptShare` 0.65 of the opening surplus; `accept-decay`: it is worth more than our next offer discounted by `acceptLookahead` rounds of decay); a single concession to a silent rival after `silentWaitTicks` (rule `silent-concede`) and never two without a counter-offer (`concessionsSinceRival` of `schemas.ts`, also in the endgame); repeating the same offer to a silent rival is not sent; the "match" after no_offer checks `withinLimit` and always carries `days` if the duel negotiates them. `decay_per_round` is read from the duel (if missing, `decay` 0.08). Offline replay of the practice: early acceptance comes out neutral (407 vs 415 P); the expected gain is in the silent rivals.

Micro-concession (US-005): repeating the price does not move the rival ("20→20→20 is not a move"); if the planned price equals our last offer and the rival moved, `decideDuel` lowers (seller) or raises (buyer) by 1 P with template `counter`, without crossing `your_limit` or going below `minSurplus`; only if 1 P no longer fits is "stays at" (`hold`) used. With days, only the price changes.

## Duels v3 (3 Oct, after Duels I)

Measured in Duels I (session 2): 30 duels, 24 deals, 355 P; 15 of the 18 no-deal duels of the day were with a rival who never offered, and 4 of the 19 silent rivals accepted an offer of ours without replying. Without rival offers no rounds are counted (`rounds` 0), so conceding costs no decay. Changes:

- **Ladder for silent rivals**: to a rival who never offered, up to `silentLadder` (3) concessions along the curve, one every `silentWaitTicks`; in the last `endgameTicks`, one more step at `silentEndgameShare` (0.5) of the floor. With a rival who has already offered, the rule of a single concession without a counter-offer still applies. Guardrail in `test/duels-micro-step.test.ts`.
- **Lower anchor** (hint 3: *open with an offer the other side can take*): `anchorMargin` 0.5 → 0.35. The floor and `acceptShare` are measured against `referenceMargin` (0.5) so that opening lower does not also lower the floor or the acceptance threshold.
- **Day deadlock** (`dayStand`, rule `day-stand`; Duels II, approved by Pablo): if the rival's last two offers ask for the same day, which is not ours, their offer there falls below `minSurplus` and their own price on our best day would already give us a deal, we offer our best day at the curve's surplus. We never concede beyond their price, never ask for more surplus than the previous offer, and it only applies if the day matters (≥ `dayStandShare` 0.2 of the reference surplus). Cases: 5659 and 5679 (buyer, the rival fixed on day 10 and no deal), which would have offered 131 / day 0 and 40 / day 0. Guardrail in `test/duels-days.test.ts`.
- **Duels III (4 Oct, pending approval until Pablo says yes):** (a) **hold the day** (`dayHold`, rule `day-hold`): before the endgame, if our day range is worth ≥ `dayHoldShare` (0.4) of the reference surplus, we always offer our best day and only concede price (Payday tip 5; 6068 gave up 42 P of day for 15 of price), never beyond the rival's price on that day; in the endgame the day split returns. (b) `dayStand` also with **a single offer** from the rival if their price on our day already gives at least the floor (6150: 55 P on day 10 = +31.7).
- **Last move at 2 ticks** (`lastMoveTicks` 1 → 2, 4 Oct, after Duels III): with 2 ticks or fewer, any rival offer that leaves ≥ `minSurplus` is accepted. The play loop (~30 s with 15 s ticks) saw 11146 only with 10, 8, 6, 4 and 2 ticks remaining and waited without accepting 108 P / day 0 (+3): no deal. See also `--duels-fast` in [`src/coordinator/`](../coordinator/AGENTS.md).
- **Measured in Duels III (session 4, 4 Oct):** 68 duels, 55 deals, ~+1300 P (seller 27.9 average per deal, buyer 21.5). A 1-round deal left 32.2 on average; 4–5 rounds, 12–14. The rival accepted our offer in 31 of the 55 deals. 15 rivals never spoke: 8 accepted our ladder and 7 did not. 4 of the 13 no-deals were lost to cadence, ~93 P (≈ 7 %): **11594** (the rival moved to our 138 / day 10, +79.5, at tick 1989; play did not look at the duel again until 1990 and it ended after the deadline of 1991, ~64 P), 11518 (~15), 11540 (84 / day 0 = +16 with 2 ticks, counter-offer instead of accepting, ~12) and 11146 (~2). Held day (`dayHold`/`dayStand`): 11627 (+32.4) and 11137 (+21.1) closed on our day. Fixes: last move at 2 ticks and `--duels-fast`.
- **Endgame with the rival outside the limit on price only** (Grand Final, 4 Oct, approved by Pablo): in 15824 (seller, limit 100, 6.66 P per day, rival at 62 / day 10) and 15838 the endgame offered 101 / day 0 (+1) instead of 100 / day 10 (+66.6), and there was no deal. The price cut in `offerForSurplus` left day 0 as the only exact day. Now, in the endgame, our limit price is offered on the rival's day if it leaves ≥ `minSurplus` and the price is no worse for them than our previous offer. We still do not accept a price that crosses `your_limit`, even if the days compensate. Guardrail in `test/duels-days.test.ts`.
- **`--restart-check`** (`pnpm bazaar:duels --restart-check [--restart-ticks 5]`, GET only): exits with 1 if a live duel ends in ≤ 5 ticks. Before restarting the coordinator: 2402 lost ~11 P by being down 5 ticks in its endgame.

**Assumption**: `your_days_weight` is read as P per day (number or `{ weight }`) or a table per day (array or object "0".."10"); with a number, `days_meaning` fixes the direction if it is clear (*cost*, *delay*, *earlier* → fewer days; *later is better* → more), otherwise its sign gives it. No duel with days has been seen yet, so the schema accepts any shape and a duel with days whose weight is not understood **is paused** (rule `days-unreadable`: no message and no acceptance, warning in the log with the JSON received) instead of playing as if days were worth 0; only `pnpm bazaar:duels --assumed-days-weight N` plays it with that weight. A duel with `your_days_weight` or `days_meaning` counts as two-issue even if `issues` is missing. Guardrail in `test/duels-days.test.ts`.
