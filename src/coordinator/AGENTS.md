# src/coordinator/ — Coordinator

`pnpm bazaar:play`: each tick it builds the state, checks the agenda and triggers, asks each route for intents without sending them, and arbitrates with the `clock.limits` budget. Live only without --dry-run and with --confirm.

## Files

- **`coordinator.ts`** — pure. `budgetFrom` reads the budget from `clock.limits` (accepts per tick, messages per conversation, open threads, new listings per tick, open offers); a missing limit counts as 0 and a warning is raised. `ACCEPT_PRIORITY`: duel (the value decays every round) > SAL-09 or a purchase that completes a page > dispersion scanner (`scanner`, markets route) > dealer ladder > the rest; at equal rank, higher expected value first. `arbitrate`: at most N accepts across all routes, messages per conversation (none where an accept goes out), new threads and new listings within the quotas, and **one asset in one place** also across routes in the same tick (`locks`). Duel accepts go **outside the team quota** (official duels PDF: *duel messages and accepts have their own limits: they never block your trading*): one per duel and tick (`DUEL_ACCEPT_QUOTA_ASSUMPTION`, also in the output).
- **`arbitrage.ts`** — arbitrage between personas, proposal only: `personaArbitrage` looks for rarities where the measured floor of a selling persona (hi of her `sells:` band) is below the measured ceiling of another buying persona (her `buys:` band), with a margin ≥ max(1 P, 5 % of book); `arbitrageLines` checks them against the thread quota left by what is already selected (two are needed) and the agendas that personas close. It is never executed and never spends quota; the purchased card only enters the locks once it exists. Figures come from the per-persona fit, never from a text.
- **`routes.ts`** — `DuelsRoute`, `DealersRoute` and `TradesRoute`: they propose without sending (propose method) and, live, execute only what was selected (execute method). `TradesRoute` uses the coordinator's `--max-spend` and `--cash-floor` as El Rastro's cap and floor. Its sell listings carry the `sell:<card>` lock (like the markets route's sales) only on the first of each card in the tick; another duplicate copy carries `sell:<card>#n` and its own asset, so its sibling does not drop it. The cancellation of a reprice only goes out if its new listing goes out (otherwise the old one stays published), and they are not proposed if the markets route would sell that card this tick to a higher bid (`marketSale`; note «list … held back: bid …»; with `--scanner`, `TradesRoute.scannerContext` hands it the same ledger, floor and cap as the markets route, plus the targeted sales and `--page-targets`, so it only holds back what the scanner will really sell). Dealers: one `BazaarAgent` per unlocked dealer (like serious mode: mandatory sheet, per-dealer profile, safety 1.0, `TeamBudget`) with a `gate`: the first pass collects and denies, the second opens the gate only to what was selected. `FlagsRoute`: proposes one flag per verifiable flagCandidate not yet sent; a pressure phrase (`tactic`) only if approved (`FlagApproval`), otherwise it is listed as a candidate; it does not use the accept quota. **Egg probes riding on a counteroffer** (`eggProbeFor`, `EGG_PARAMS`): `DealersRoute` hands the agent the phrase X and the template appends «Do you know about X?» to a counteroffer we were already going to send; never a separate message or thread. At most one per conversation (plus one retry in Spanish if the first was in English; within each rank the Spanish phrases go first, `isSpanishPhrase`: Abuela's egg fired with «la chulapa dorada» and «the Moscow gold» failed with Chato), never the same X with the same persona (`eggsTried` and `eggProbes`), nor to a persona where one of our eggs already fired (they are once per team). **One-use greeting** (`EGG_PARAMS.greetings`, `GREETINGS`): «¡Felicidades por su santo, Abuela!» (congratulations on your saint's day, Abuela) goes once a day in the next counteroffer with Abuela, because of the news about her saint's day (approved by Pablo); it takes priority over the probe and goes out even if her egg already fired, never with warnings, strikes or cooloff, X without digits (`isProbePhrase`). `EggsRoute` only reports which probe would go with the next counteroffer, and the one waiting for a persona with no open thread («chato: queued "Do you know about the Moscow gold?" (no open thread…)»): it goes out with the next counteroffer in that thread, never in a separate message. **Unlock chase** (`unlockChaseFor`): to each agent it passes the active, not unlocked persona whose early_deals_with is that dealer and whose early_min_level we reach (hypothesis: the server only evaluates the unlock when closing a deal with that dealer); the agent's `unlock-chase …` lines go out as tick notes. **Packs**: it passes `packValueOf` (our estimated value from `GameState.packs`), so each dealer's agent also negotiates `{buy: {pack}}`. `DealersRoute` also passes the agent the cap from each conversation's expected limit (*prediction* of the per-persona fit, `offerCap`); **Lessons**: each agent `ThreadSummary` goes through `PendingLessons` (the same functions as serious mode); live, `appendLesson` writes `docs/bazaar/lessons.json` (`lessonsFile`), in dry-run it only prints «lessons (dry-run, nothing written): would append…» and does not touch `docs/`; `flushLessons` when finishing with `--once`. **Big first concession per persona** (`MIRROR_FIRST_STEP_FRAC` 0.12, `firstStepFrac` of the agent): on only if `model.strategy.mirror_concessions` is true in that persona's `PersonaModel` (and `herBeta` = `model.strategy.beta`: with β < 1 the agent does not apply the fixed price) (`personaArbitrage` also reads `model.bands`) (Chato; not Abuela: her curve already explains it), off otherwise; guardrails, monotonicity and the reserve still rule. `main.ts` loads and saves the posterior (`loadPosterior`, `savePosterior`). **Buy early in the hour** (*demand_markup*: her limit rises as the hour's stock runs out): the value of buy openings to a dealer is weighted with `demandWeight` (×1.25 at the start of the game hour, ×0.75 at the end), so arbitrage prefers them at the start. **Page reserve** (`pageReserveOf`, `pageTargetCaps`): incoming cash is kept aside for each page target we still lack (SAL-09) up to its cap (`pageTargetCap`: its base without bonus, or 0.9 × `your_value` with `--page-bonus-scored`; if a dealer already asked for less, her last price); only purchases for that target use it, and other purchases of page cards rank above (in El Rastro, accepts and bids that bring a page card we lack; in dealers, `TeamBudget.pageReserve`). A purchase without a new page card does not touch it. Tick note: «page reserve: 91 P kept for SAL-09 (SAL-09 91 P = cap 91; …)».
- **`score-audit.ts`** — per-deal scoring audit (GET only), measured by `DealersRoute` each tick with `ScoreAudit`: from the tick feed (`GameState.events`) it reads our settlements since the last read (`ourDeals`), each with its origin (`dealer` if the settlement carries a persona, `team` if it is a venue), counterparty, side, cards, price and our `your_value` **before** the deal (what was sold, from the previous tick's `/api/me` by asset id; what was bought, from the client's private-value cache), and compares Σ expected (buy v − p, sale p − v, no commission) with the Δ of `neg_points`. Verdict (`auditVerdict`): `match` if |Δ − expected| ≤ 1; `dealer-unscored` if there are only dealer deals, Δ = 0 and expected ≠ 0; `mismatch` otherwise (`missingValue` if a value was missing). It shows up in `play.log` as «score audit: neg_points a → b (Δ …) at tick N: verdict · expected … · deals: dealer chato buy RET-10 @ 91 P (v 112, exp +21, tick 530)» and, live only, one record per tick with a Δ or with deals of ours in `results/bazaar-live/<date>/score-audit.jsonl` (`defaultScoreAuditFile`); in dry-run only the line. Each record also carries `parts`: the Δ of each score part (`SCORE_PARTS`: score, negotiating, market, neg_points, ladder_points, duel_points, mm_points) in that tick, because a dealer deal scores through `ladder_points`, not `neg_points`; a tick with a ladder-only Δ is also written. The viewer shows it on each deal (column «Points»). The first read only sets the baseline. Reason: RET-10 bought from El Chato for 91 P at tick 530 left `neg_points` at 69.1 (Δ 0).
- **`plan-log.ts`** — `plan.jsonl`: one line per tick, written after executing, in `results/bazaar-live/<date>/plan.jsonl` (the same date as `FileTrace`). Zod schema `PlanLineSchema` (exported for the auditor, pnpm bazaar:audit): `{v: 1, tick, ts, mode, cash, cashFloor, maxSpend, holdings, intents, arbitration, execution, routeErrors?}`. `holdings` counts copies per card (packs as `pack:<id>`); `intents` carries id, route, type, card and assets (from the `locks`), figure, expected value and `ACCEPT_PRIORITY` rank; `arbitration`, SELECTED/DROPPED with its reason; `execution`, one entry per line of `== execution ==` (`ok: false` and `error` with the code after «failed:», e.g. offer_not_open), tied to the selected intent whose id it mentions. If writing fails, it only warns («plan: not written») and the tick goes on.
- **`main.ts`** — `pnpm bazaar:play --dry-run --once`: prints the `GameState` (with TIME, eggs, gifts and flags), the next 5 agenda events with countdown and planned action, the tick's triggers, the hint count per persona with the 3 latest candidates, one line per persona with status and unlock progress, the budget, each proposed intent, what goes out and what does not (and why), one line per conversation (type, counterparty, asset, phase, latest prices, turn, last decision, planned path) and the detail of each duel. Live only without `--dry-run` **and** with `--confirm` (without `--confirm` it runs as dry-run); with the clock paused or doors closed it waits. Flags: `--max-spend-hour` (60), `--max-spend` (150), `--cash-floor` (20: the venue is already paid for), `--page-targets` (SAL-09: the only page target; outside it a page card is worth its loose surplus, no bonus), `--page-bonus-scored` (off: a page target is capped at its base without bonus, SAL-09 ≈ 91 P of 177.1, in El Rastro, markets and dealers; with it, 0.9 × `your_value`; turn it on only when `score-audit` shows the bonus scores), `--leaderboard-every` (5), `--approve-flags` (message ids with an approved pressure phrase), `--flag-pressure` (all approved) `--allow-venue-switch` (with `--confirm`, approves the venue mechanism change) and `--rival-page` (with `--confirm`, sends the targeted sales of [`src/markets/rival-page.ts`](../markets/AGENTS.md); without it, only «would POST» lines; the markets route calls `proposeRivalPage` with the tick's state and El Rastro plan, `lastState` and `lastPlan` of `TradesRoute`), `--scanner` (opt-in, off by default: without it the markets route keeps its previous rules, first-copy value and net ≥ 1) and `--scanner-spend-per-hour` (60: the dispersion scanner's purchase cap per game hour, in memory during the run; `ScannerLedger` shared between `proposeMarkets` and `executeMarkets`; the markets route receives `lastState` from `TradesRoute` as the marginal-value model and does not sell cards with a rival-page targeted sale), `--rival-buy` (same for the targeted bids of [`src/markets/rival-buy.ts`](../markets/AGENTS.md), with `proposeRivalBuy` and the same `--max-spend` as El Rastro), `--workshop` (opt-in: without it, the `workshop` route of [`src/workshop/`](../workshop/AGENTS.md) only leaves the line «workshop: would craft …»; with it and `--confirm`, at most one POST /api/taller per tick, after rechecking the guardrails; it only uses duplicates with no team demand and never cancels a listing for El Taller; `arbitrate` drops the craft if another intent sells or lists the same copy or card in that tick), `--rival-swap` (opt-in: without it nothing is proposed; with it, targeted card-for-card swaps from [`src/markets/rival-swap.ts`](../markets/AGENTS.md) with `proposeRivalSwap`, sent only with `--confirm`, and El Rastro accepts swaps targeted at us without a card that closes a page); `--duels-fast` (opt-in, for the Grand Final: duels take one step per tick in their own loop, runFast method of `DuelsRoute`, with the same client and the same 4 req/s bucket; the main loop no longer proposes them. Reason: a full play tick takes ~30 s with 15 s ticks and in Duels III we only moved one tick out of two; `[duels] [fast]` lines in `play.log`; with `--once`, a single step); `--plan-log <path>` writes `plan.jsonl` at that path, also in dry-run (without it, only live). `venue-mechanism` route (from [`src/venue/`](../venue/AGENTS.md)): fills `GameState.venue.mechanismDecision` with the shadow broker's sessions and its heartbeat, prints a line «venue: auto · bench 2/2 measured · shadow/auto 1.14 → …» and proposes `venue:switch-to-board` or `venue:back-to-auto` (`arbitrate`: at most one per tick).
