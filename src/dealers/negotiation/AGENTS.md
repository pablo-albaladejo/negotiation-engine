# src/dealers/negotiation/ — Negotiating with a dealer

The figure of each message to a dealer and the guardrails before accepting. Pure: no network or I/O.

## Files

- **`negotiator.ts`** — `decide`: pure and deterministic. Default mode adaptive (`stepMode`), tuned to the real threads 56 and 125: her patience is spent per exchange (~6–7 messages of ours, not per tick), she concedes much less than we do and a distant anchor only burns patience. Moderate anchor: when buying `buyAnchorFrac` = 0.75× her opening, below our limit; when selling, if her bid does not cover our minimum, ⌈minimum × `sellFloorAnchorMult` (1.3)⌉; if it does, `sellAnchorMult` (2.0) × her bid trimmed to close within her patience; in sales the anchor never exceeds `sellAnchorCapMult` (1.3) × her list for that rarity (no list, no cap). Step (`adaptiveStep`) = ⌈gap to our limit ÷ remaining patience (`patienceBudget` = 6 messages)⌉ between 1 and `maxStep` (3); `stepResponses` measures her response to each step and `bigStepsDidNotPay` goes back to steps of 1 if a step ≥ 2 did not get more out of her than one of 1 (rule `adaptive-fallback`). **Big first concession, off by default and turned on per persona** (`firstStepFrac` 0, rule `mirror-first-step`; `--first-step-frac 0.12` forces it in `pnpm bazaar`; `bazaar:play` turns it on (`MIRROR_FIRST_STEP_FRAC` 0.12) only against a persona with `mirror_concessions` true (e.g. Chato), see [coordinator](../../coordinator/AGENTS.md); the offline replay gave the same on the saved threads and worse on synthetic rare/epic ones): against the dealer's *mirror_concessions* (she never concedes faster than our last step), the first step after the anchor is at least 12 % of the anchor → effective reserve stretch, even if it exceeds `maxStep`; then the usual curve. `mirrorVerdict` classifies her responses: *mirror* if a big step got more out of her than those of 1, *not-mirror* if not (goes in the trace as `mirror`; back to steps of 1, `adaptive-fallback`; thread 257: we dropped 3 P and she did not move). `plannedSchedule` gives the planned path. The boulware mode (`LEGACY_NEGOTIATOR_PARAMS`: anchor 0.45 / 2.0×, the engine's `concession` curve with β 0.5 and reciprocity) stays for comparison. **First conversation with a new dealer** (*welcome_first_deal*, site-map § 8.2: her opening is her limit): if her price fits in the private reserve and creates value at our private value (EV > 0, `welcomeTake`), before any other rule ONE counteroffer 1 P better for us goes out (`welcome-counter`: accepting the opening without offering counts as took_opening, not as negotiated) and, since the dealer cannot pass her limit and repeats her price, we accept it (`welcome-first-deal`); in that conversation it replaces the «do not close at her opening» of `effectiveReservation`; **her price is no longer taken at the first go** (thread 894: Pilar 47 → 48 → 50 and we accepted at 50, share ≈ 0.46, ladder +0.014): it is only accepted with a `welcomeTakeReason` (`final` offer, share ≥ `welcomeTakeShare` 0.8 of her range up to `herLimitCap`, she stood still after our counteroffer or our next one would pass her `walk_after_rounds` from the persona model, Pilar 4); otherwise we keep negotiating towards the anchor (replay of 894: 64, 61, 58, 55 → her final 53); `WELCOME_FIRST_DEAL_PAST` (abuela, chato) disables it for dealers already seen. Always: monotonicity and mandate with `enforceGuardrails`, AC_next to accept, never repeats a price, one message per thread and tick, rule `opening-last-chance`: if her opening still has not moved and she is about to leave (`final` offer or our message no. `patienceBudget` − 1) and it creates value, it accepts it. **Fixed price** (`fixedAfterConcessions` = 2, learned in thread 56), only when the dealer buys from us (when she sells to us she does concede, thread 178: we negotiate until her final): if after 2 concessions of ours her price has not moved, it accepts if it fits in the private reserve and creates value at our private value (`privateValue`), even if it is her opening (the deal counts), and otherwise closes politely (`fixed-price` / `fixed-price-out-of-range`). Only against a persona who concedes early: with her β (`herBeta`, from `PersonaModel.strategy.beta`) below `fixedPriceMinBeta` (1) it does not apply, because not moving at the start is what her curve predicts (El Chato, β ≈ 0.35: 4 % of the way in round 2); with no known β, it applies as usual. Outside that it does not accept her opening except as a `final` offer within the reserve and with positive value; a `final` outside the reserve closes. If our next counteroffer would cross the reserve: if her price fits in our limit and creates value, it accepts (`stuck-accept-within-limit`); otherwise it holds the same price (`hold`, text only) at most `maxHolds` (1) time and never right after another message with no offer (*lastWasTextOnly*; the farewell is not sent after a hold either), and closes (`holds-exhausted`): in thread 184 holding for 6 ticks did not bring her final, she left with no_progress. **Lowball** (`isLowball`, `lowballFrac` 0.7): after a counteroffer, if her first price and the current one stay below 0.7 × our minimum when selling (thread 125: 5–6 for a minimum of 10) or above our maximum ÷ 0.7 when buying (e.g. when revealing a duplicate), it closes politely (`lowball-bid`). **Cap by her expected limit** (*herLimitCap*, from `offerCap` in [`src/dealers/history/`](../history/AGENTS.md)): when buying, we never offer above her expected limit plus a margin (max of 1 P and 2 % of the book price); when selling, we never ask below her expected limit minus that margin; it only narrows the offer's reserve, never loosens it, and acceptance still uses the private reserve. The parameters are configured through `AgentOptions.negotiator` (flags of `pnpm bazaar`: anchors, holds, patience, step, mode and first step) and `negotiatorForDealer` ([`../dealer-profile.ts`](../AGENTS.md)) adjusts them per dealer (patience by traits; Chato: patience 8, step 1, no holds, sell anchor 1.7×).
- **`patience.ts`** — `PatienceLog`: per conversation, our messages, her responses, ticks until her final offer (or the close) and her response to each step of ours; it goes in the trace (`patience`) on accept, close and finish, and `formatPatience` prints the line "patience: 6 msgs / 7 ticks until final · …".
- **`offer-structure.ts`** — `checkStructure`: **critical guardrail** before any accept, the shape of her offer must be that of the thread (sale: cash > 0 for exactly our assets; purchase: exactly the requested card for cash only ≤ limit). `firstMismatch` checks all her offers: if she sells us something in a sale (pack, card) or asks for other assets, the agent closes politely with the rule `structure-mismatch` and never accepts.
- **`view.ts`** — `threadPrices`: reads from the thread only structured fields (prices of messages and standing offers), never her text.
- **`messages.ts`** — friendly templates in English (all with *please* or *thank*: courtesy lowers the dealer's limit, site-map § 9.2), asserting nothing false and without phrases from `TEMPLATE_BLOCKLIST` (`hasBlockedPhrase`: ignore, instruction, last one, other offer, deadline, lowest price…), rotated by round; `textMatchesPrice` requires that the only figure in the text is the price. `counterText` supports an egg probe: it appends «Do you know about X?» only if X is valid (`isProbePhrase`: no digits), so the figure is still one. If X is in `GREETINGS` (closed list of one-use greetings, today «¡Felicidades por su santo, Abuela!» — congratulations on your saint's day, Abuela), it appends the greeting as is instead of the question; a greeting can also go in `holdText` (a hold-price message we were already going to send), an egg question cannot. `closeText` (the farewell, no figure) carries the pending probe, question or greeting: the Pícaros close with a lowball in round 1 and never see a second counteroffer (thread 1067, tick 769: the Moscow gold probe stayed queued and did not go out).
