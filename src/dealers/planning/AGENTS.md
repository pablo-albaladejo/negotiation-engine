# src/dealers/planning/ — What to negotiate with each dealer

Picks purchases and sales per dealer and their reserve; the figure of each message is decided by [`negotiation/`](../negotiation/AGENTS.md).

## Files

- **Blind buys off by default** (`RankInput.blindBuys`, 3 Oct): neither rarity+set (she picks the card: threads 519+523 bought duplicate SAL-06 and SAL-08, `neg_points` −39.8 = (8.1 − 24) + (8.1 − 32)) nor packs (what comes out is *luck* and does not score). Page gaps are requested as a concrete card `{buy: {card}}`. **Card that completes a page** (`pageCompleting`): limit = value × `PAGE_COMPLETING_SAFETY` (0.9) trimmed only by cash above the floor (`pageBudget` in `rankCandidates` and in `selectCandidates`), not by the hourly cap: with 60 P/h no rare (list 77) had room and SAL-09 (value 177.1, with the page bonus) went unrequested. The only copy of a page ≥ 50 % complete (`ONLY_COPY_PAGE_COMPLETE_BLOCK`) is not sold only if it belongs to a page-target set (`pageTargets`: SAL) or to a complete page; in other sets its `your_value` is already the reserve and +5 P (`ONLY_COPY_MIN_SURPLUS`) is enough: the album does not score by itself (so Doña Pilar, who had no target, gets LAT-10 and LAT-08).
- **`plan.ts`** — menu-based planner (the one the loop uses if it has the dealer's profile): `rankCandidates` orders purchases from the dealer by value to us (concrete card: `/api/me/value`, also cards we lack on a rarity+set entry with topic `{buy: {card}}` (not yet tried with the dealer); rarity+set: expected value over the cards of that rarity and set, already marginal (those we already have, at their duplicate value: `nextCopyValue`; so buying a concrete missing card beats rarity+set as soon as some card of the set is already in hand), and it is discarded if P(duplicate) > `MAX_DUPLICATE_P` and the worst-case loss > `MAX_WORST_CASE_LOSS`, or if it does not exceed her list by `RARITY_SET_MARGIN`), reserve = value × `safety` (0.9 by default; `--safety`, 1.0 in serious mode) trimmed by spend and cash, and there is only room if the reserve exceeds her list. **Card that would complete a page**: its `/api/me/value` carries the page bonus, and the album does not score by itself, so it is worth its base without bonus (`buildValueModel`) unless it is a page target (`RankInput.pageTargets`, SAL-09) with `RankInput.pageBonusScored` (`--page-bonus-scored`), which keeps the bonus with `PAGE_COMPLETING_SAFETY` (0.9). A target without the flag is capped at its base (SAL-09 ≈ 91 P, not 0.9 × 177.1) but still uses cash above the floor and the page reserve; a card that would complete another page is just another purchase (no page budget). Without `pageTargets` (plain `pnpm bazaar`) no card counts the bonus. If no purchase has room, sales of what she buys: **never sell the only copy of a card on a page ≥ 50 % complete of a page-target or complete set**; the others yes, with +5 P; her bid is unknown until her first price in the thread (13 once and 5–6 another time for the same common), so it is only opened if her plausible ceiling (`PLAUSIBLE_BID_FRAC` × her sell list for that rarity; with no list, like Pilar, `MEASURED_BID_CEILING`: uncommon 25 and epic 140 measured, rare 70 = her book) reaches our minimum, reserve = value ÷ `safety`; each sale says whether it is a DUPLICATE or our ONLY copy and its impact on the page; order: duplicates, lowest impact, most value created. Sale anchor capped at 1.3× the dealer's list price. `selectCandidates` prefers a common and an uncommon if both have room; with `--only` (`parseOnly`/`applyOnly`, e.g. `buy:uncommon:SAL,buy:common:SAL`) it opens only those, in that order and without requiring room over her list (the negotiator still does not exceed the limit). `previewPath` and `formatPlan` give the dry-run plan (value, limit, list/opening, anchor, expected path within patience, path if she does not move and first message with her price). `menuBlocks`: menu guard (only what her menu buys or sells). **Packs** (`buy-pack`, only if the agent receives `packValueOf`): for each `pack` entry of her `menu.sells`, topic `{buy: {pack}}` (shape from the kit and from real threads; her offer gives `types: ["pack:sobre_barrio"]`), reserve = our estimated value × `PACK_SAFETY` (0.97) trimmed by spend and cash, room if it exceeds her list and the list is under her opening; competes with cards by value created. `chaseCandidates` (unlock chase, no room): duplicates she buys with plausible ceiling ≥ value − 2 and concrete-card purchases (or rarity+set without duplicate risk) with list ≤ value + 2; first those with value created ≥ 0, duplicates before purchases; reserve = value ± `UNLOCK_CHASE_TOLERANCE`.
- **`forex.ts`** — arbitrage between dealers (Pablo's decision, 3 Oct): the routes are the dealer-to-dealer chains that [`src/forex/`](../../forex/AGENTS.md) finds each tick (the coordinator passes them with `setForexRoutes`; `forexRoutes` reads them); until then, `FOREX_SEED_ROUTES`: a Salamanca rare (SAL-09, SAL-10) to the Pícaros for ≤ 56 and that copy to Pilar for ≥ 70 (public medians: Pícaros ~54, Pilar ~75). A route whose copy we still hold continues until it is sold. Deals with dealers pay no fee (fee 0 in today's 413 settlements) and El Rastro charges 2–9 P: a route is only worth it if `forexMargin` (sale − purchase − fees) ≥ `FOREX_MIN_MARGIN`. At most `FOREX_MAX_LOTS` unsold copies (`forexLots`: every copy beyond the first); our first copy is never resold. In `plan.ts` the purchase does not require bidding above her list and the resale uses the route's minimum (not `your_value`); the agent does not discard it by the rarity band and the coordinator opens it even if it does not win the ladder.
- **`planner.ts`** — old planner (without the dealer's profile): `spareTargets` (duplicates, reserve = her your_value) and `buyTargets` (missing page cards, reserve = your_value × the agent's `safety` (0.85 by default), trimmed by hourly budget and cash). The loop uses it only without the dealer's profile (and not with `requireMenu`) (`buyTargets`, `raritySetTargets`, `missingPageCards`); `bazaar:status` and the `GameState` only use `spareTargets` (and `rarityOf`).
