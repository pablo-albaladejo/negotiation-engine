# src/trades/ — El Rastro

Trades with other teams at El Rastro (5 % fee + 1 P per card, rounded up), scored at OUR private values. Only the structure of each offer (give/want) is read, never its text. Entry: `main.ts` (`pnpm bazaar:trades`).

## Files

- **`trades.ts`** — pure and deterministic. Valuation: `buildValueModel` derives each card's private base from `your_value` (÷ marginal of the last copy; duplicates 0.25× and 0.1× according to the catalog's copy marginals; on a complete page the `your_value` of a single copy also carries the page bonus that would be lost, and it is discounted: RET common 122 = base 16 + bonus 106, and a second copy is worth 4, not 30.5) and from `/api/me/value` for the ones we do not have (without double-counting the bonus if that card would complete the page); `portfolioValue` adds copies + page and set bonus; `pageRisk` penalises removing our only copy from an almost complete page (≥ 8/10). `tradeFee`, `minAsk` (sale floor = loss + fee + margin) and `maxBid` (bid ceiling = value − fee − margin). **El Rastro's fee is paid only by whoever accepts** (checked against our cash, ticks 320–380): `evaluateOffer` charges it with the fee of the offer's venue (`venueFeesOf`: `fee_bps` and `fee_per_card` from `/api/venues`, which `agent.ts` reads every tick with `parseVenueFees`; El Rastro and an unknown venue, `params.fees` = `RASTRO_FEES`); accepting in another venue is the same `POST /api/offers/{id}/accept`; on our own listings and bids we are the publisher and `planTick` uses `MAKER_FEES` (0). `evaluateOffer` gives the value created if we accept (cards + cash − fee − risk, counting only free copies: those locked elsewhere or reserved count as gone, `acceptLockedIds`; those in our own El Rastro listings do not, because that listing is cancelled in the same tick, before accepting) and requires a margin (2 P or 10 % of the cash); it never pays with the last free copy of a card (`takesLastFreeCopy`, reason `last-free-copy`). `planTick`: one acceptance per tick (the most valuable one that fits in cash and in `--max-spend`), duplicates for sale with a single price per card and tick: if there are rival asks for that card, the cheapest − 1 P with no premium; otherwise the reference + 5 % (copies locked or busy elsewhere count as already gone: a free copy always remains for the album, and a listing that would leave none is cancelled) (`priceReference`: feed settlements, `parseSettlements`, per card and then per rarity; otherwise the median of what is asked on the board for that card; otherwise the lower quartile of the asks of its rarity with ≥ 3 (`RARITY_ASKS_MIN`); otherwise book; a per-rarity reference carries no premium) and never below the floor (the highest among the card's duplicate copies); bids for missing page cards (best bid + 1 or 0.9× the reference, never above the ceiling; first those of target pages that lack the least, then the others by value). **The album does not score by itself** (score = negotiating + market): outside `--page-targets` a page card is worth its loose surplus (`buyGain`: base × marginal, without the page bonus that `valueDelta` would add on completing it; `unscoredPageBonus`), in bids and in acceptances; so no bid for LAT, MAL or LAV comes out just to advance a page. A page target (SAL-09) is capped at its base without bonus (`pageTargetCap`, ≈ 91 P of 177.1) as long as `--page-bonus-scored` is not passed (`scoredPageSets`); with it, its bonus counts and at most `PAGE_BONUS_SCORED_FRAC` (0.9) × its value is paid, in bids and acceptances. Plan note: «page target SAL-09: bid cap 89 P (base 91.0, page bonus not counted)». The page reserve (`pageReserve`, set by the coordinator) only holds back purchases that bring a page card we are missing; those for the target may use it; our own directed offers (with `to`, those of `src/markets/rival-page.ts`) are neither cancelled nor repriced here, they count as open and their asset is busy; a listing is repriced directly to its card's price only if it moved ≥ `repriceHysteresis` (2 P), a rival asks less or there is another copy of the card at another price; bids with slow repricing (`slowReprice`, offers of ≥ 10 ticks, steps of 5 %); immediate correction if an offer ends up below the floor or above the ceiling, and cancellation of invalid ones. **No passive bids** (3 Oct: 162 bids, 0 filled): by default no bid is published at El Rastro (open ones are cancelled) and a purchase from the book is accepted only if it brings a card that closes a page (target or the last one missing); `--rastro-bids` (`rastroBids`) brings it back; with `--rival-swap` (`rivalSwap`) a card-for-card swap with no cash (only the fee) is also accepted, with the same margin and the same last-free-copy rule. No listing below `minListPrice` (4 P; the publisher pays no fee, so this only avoids pocket-change listings; a sale to a team scores price − value and one to a dealer, 0). A structured offer another team makes to us inside a team thread on a venue is evaluated like a directed one (same value, margin and last-copy rule; accepted by its structure alone). Each offer directed at us leaves a line `to-me #id …` with what we receive, what we give, the value and the verdict, to audit lost deals. The venue-switch reserve (`venueReserve`, set by the coordinator) is used only by purchases that close a page. A card published `relistBackoffAfter` (2) times (listings and reprices, adding all its copies) without selling is neither repriced nor republished for `relistBackoffTicks` (60) ticks; the counter is kept in `results/bazaar-live/list-backoff.json` (`defaultListBackoffFile`, written only live) because `pnpm bazaar:play` restarts often and in memory it never reached the limit (LAT-04 #393 published 34 times). Caps: `--max-offers`, `--max-bids`, `--max-spend` (accepted purchases + open bids with fee), `--cash-floor` (cash that is never spent), server limits (`/api/clock` → limits). With `TradeState.rivals` (`RivalSignals`, which in `pnpm bazaar:play` `TradesRoute` sets with `tradeSignals` from `src/state/rivals.ts`): a duplicate that another team asked for or that leaves it at ≤ 2 from a page is listed a `demandPremium` (15 %) higher (not if there are rival asks for that card or if the reference is per rarity), never below the floor; and among bids with the same distance to a page, the cards a rival held in the last 60 ticks go first (such a bid can fill). Without that data the plan does not change; a «rivals: …» note summarises it. A bid of ours that fills counts toward spend only at its price. Our open bids for a card in `foreignBidRefs` (the open epic lane of `src/markets/rival-buy.ts`) belong to that route: here they are neither cancelled nor repriced, and do not count as spend. **Publishing venue** (Pablo, 4 Oct): our listings and bids go out only on `OFFER_VENUE` (`src/shared/offer-venue.ts`, v21: Team 9's market, 0 % fee, board); those still open at El Rastro are cancelled («moved to v21») and republished there. `agent.ts` reads El Rastro's board and that of `OFFER_VENUE`, and both can be accepted from. Payday cap per counterparty (`roomOf` and `MIN_ROOM` of `src/markets/room.ts`; `agent.ts` reads it every tick with `loadCounterpartyRoom`): an offer from a team with margin < 10 is not accepted (reason `no-room`; a loss counts in full and a gain no longer scores), and the gain used to order acceptances is trimmed to the margin it has left.
- **`agent.ts`** — `TradesAgent`, method propose (state and `planTick`, GET only) and method execute (a plan, possibly trimmed by the coordinator); `TradesAgent.step` = both: reads clock, `/api/me`, our offers, the board and the feed; caches private values for the whole run; executes cancellations → acceptance → listings (nothing in dry-run; if a cancellation fails it does not accept that tick, because paying with a copy could leave a listed copy as the last one whose cancellation is in the same plan; a backoff beyond a rest period from the current tick is discarded; a reprice is published only if its cancellation went through, and an asset_locked error reserves that asset for a few ticks); a bid of ours that disappears without being cancelled before expiring counts as spend.
- **`main.ts`** — `runTradesCli`: `pnpm bazaar:trades --dry-run --once` (GET only). Live it requires removing `--dry-run` and passing `--confirm` (only with the user's approval). Flags: `--max-offers` (16), `--max-spend` (60), `--max-bids` (12), `--cash-floor` (0), `--min-margin` (2), `--expires` (40 ticks), `--page-targets` (SAL-09), `--page-bonus-scored` (as in `pnpm bazaar:play`), `--ticks`, `--top`.
