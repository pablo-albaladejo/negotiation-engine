# src/markets/ — Markets

Markets route between El Rastro and other teams' venues: net gap per venue = gap − fee − rival penalty; on a tie, El Rastro.

## Files

- **`markets.ts`** — `proposeMarkets`, `bestVenue` and `executeMarkets`. Venue table in `GameState.markets.venues` (owner with their rank and figure, fee, mechanism, status, depth; ours, can't trade) and bid/ask per venue in the price sheet. `proposeMarkets`: for each buy (any card, duplicates too, at marginal value) or sell (only spare free copies: never the last free copy of a card, `isLastFreeCopy` from `../shared/last-copy.ts`, nor a card with a rival-page targeted sale) it picks the venue with the best net gap = gap − fee (we pay it: we accept, and El Rastro charges whoever accepts) − `RIVAL_PENALTY` (0 at El Rastro, high at top-3 venues, low in the bottom half: trading there raises the owner's Market-making and the figure is relative to the leader; and at least 20 P while the venue has hosted fewer than 6 deals (`trades` from `/api/venues`, unknown counts as fewer), because organic flow is the root of hosted value and the first deals are worth a lot to their owner: Team 14's first one gave it ≈ 1.9 points; `docs/bazaar/site-map.md` § 6.11); on a tie, El Rastro. `auto` venues: the engine crosses overlapping offers at each tick before anyone accepts, so an offer that is still open crosses with nothing and accepting it is a normal acceptance (verified: v10, tick 311, settlement `trade`). The route only accepts existing offers; our own offers are posted only at El Rastro (`src/trades/trades.ts`). Threads with a team at their venue only for large page gaps (proposal). Fair play (`fairPrice`): we do not sell below half the book or buy above double. Each sale carries the lock `sell:<card>` (besides that of its asset): only one sale of that card leaves per tick across routes, and the coordinator picks acceptances first, so the highest bid beats the cheapest El Rastro listing. Buys that complete a page keep their class and their rule (net ≥ 1); the rest go through the scanner (`scanner.ts`, class `scanner`), sorted by net before applying caps, with edge, fee, net, marginal value and reason in the summary (and in `plan.jsonl`) or in a note `[scanner] … skipped: …`. `marketSale` says which sale the route would propose for a card (El Rastro uses it so as not to list it below that); with `--scanner` it is the scanner's own decision (`proposeMarkets` with the same context returns `sales`), so it applies the same rules: targeted sale, last copy of a target set, free copy, caps and cash. With `--scanner`, copies locked in our offers or reserved count as already sold: the sale is valued (`valueDelta`, `pageRisk`) with the free copies (`freeCounts`) and the last free copy is never sold. The cash floor also deducts the page-completing buys of the same proposal; spend by other routes in the same tick is not seen here. An acceptance that fails because the offer is no longer open ("offer_not_open", "not_found") is discarded for the rest of the run (v02 #4230 kept showing a bid of 22 P).
  - Epic lanes count against `--max-spend`: price + fee ≤ `--max-spend` (coordinator, 4 Oct: RET-11 filled at 240 + 13 fee with `--max-spend` 250). If the next step does not fit, the bid stays at its price until it expires.
- **`room.ts`** — points room per counterparty. The Payday cap is per counterparty and accumulates across days. Measured on 4 Oct: t05 gave +50 with CHA-05 and then 0 when buying RET-11 from them at 240, with value 288; t13 gave +50.2 with MAL-10. That is why `loadCounterpartyRoom` reads the `score-audit.jsonl` of all days and computes the room as `COUNTERPARTY_CAP` (50) minus what has already been credited for each team. The coordinator reads it every tick and passes it to rival-buy, the scanner and team-desk. Rival-buy does not bid (and cancels whatever is open) to a team with room < `MIN_ROOM` (10). The scanner counts the edge at most up to that room. Team-desk does not counter-offer to a team with room < `MIN_ROOM`. An open bid (no `to`) does not choose a counterparty: the RET-11 one filled with t05 and scored 0.
- **`scanner.ts`** — dispersion scanner (used by `proposeMarkets` only with `--scanner`; without it, `proposeMarkets` and `marketSale` keep the previous rules). `neg_points` scores the value gained against private value (buy: v − p; sell: p − v), so each leg counts on its own; it has a per-deal cap: a deal between teams scores at most 50 (Payday), so the edge is counted as min(edge, 50) (`scoredGainCap`); the per-counterparty cap is approximated with 2 deals per counterparty and hour. The epic lanes sit just below: RET-11 at 240 → +48, SAL-11 at 185 → +49. **Marginal** value (`marginalValue`): buy = `valueDelta` of one more copy, without the page bonus of a set that does not score (`buyGain`; in `proposeMarkets` also the page-completing buys: only the `--page-targets` bonus counts with `--page-bonus-scored`, and without a model a card that would complete another page is not bought); sell = what we lose with that copy + `pageRisk` (threshold 8). The model comes from `TradesRoute.lastState` (`buildValueModel` of El Rastro, the same one rival-page uses); only for cards with known private value (in hand or `/api/me/value`). Without a model: first-copy value for what we are missing and for duplicates, and never the last copy. `scanDecision`: net = edge − fee − rival penalty ≥ max(3 P, 15 % of the price) (`SCANNER_PARAMS`); buys capped per game hour (60 P, `--scanner-spend-per-hour`; hour = game hours of the clock or tick × `tick_seconds`) and without going below `--cash-floor`; at most 2 deals per counterparty and hour (RULES.md:132; counterparty = `maker` of the offer at El Rastro; at another venue, its owner, ASSUMPTION). `ScannerLedger`: what is proposed stays pending and only counts once it is really accepted (`executeMarkets`); in memory, a restart counts from zero.
- **`rival-page.ts`** — sales targeted at a rival team that is missing exactly one card of a page (`proposeRivalPage`, `executeRivalPage`, parameters in `RIVAL_PAGE_PARAMS`). Structure only: cards seen with the team, what they asked for and their leaderboard row (`GameState.rivals`). Floor = what the copy costs us (`valueDelta` + `pageRisk`) + option on our own page (bonus × bases × (have/10)^4) + penalty for the buyer's rank (`RIVAL_PENALTY`; unknown = middle), with margin 2 and no fee, because the buyer pays it on accepting (`minAsk` with `MAKER_FEES`). Their estimated value = median of our per-set multipliers (`setMultipliers`, excluding that set) × (card book + bonus × page book). κ = feasibility × desire: `pageFeasibility` (if the leaderboard's completed pages need more unseen cards than they have, κ 0; if almost all, 0.5) × 1 if they asked for it, 0.4 if they asked for sister cards and not this one, 0.8 with no data. We ask floor + 50 % · κ · (their value − floor), capped at their value, through `enforceGuardrails` and `fairPrice`. Never from a `--page-targets` set, nor our last free copy of the card (`isLastFreeCopy`: it only sells duplicates), nor with more than 6 of our cards from that page, nor with page risk, nor an asset that is locked, reserved, in another offer or that El Rastro lists this tick; at most 2 open. Offer with `to` at El Rastro, expires at 20 ticks; at 10 it is repriced toward the floor (never below, at most 2 times) and then cancelled with a 40-tick wait. It is cancelled if the team is seen with the card, their completed pages rise, κ drops below the minimum, the data is stale (> 15 ticks), the asset gets occupied, the floor exceeds the price or the agenda freezes new listings. Intents `markets:rival:post:<team>:<asset>` (listing, asset lock) and `markets:rival:cancel:<offer>`; the coordinator arbitrates them (acceptances and El Rastro win the asset). `[rival-page] …` lines in the output of `pnpm bazaar:play`; live only with `--confirm` **and** `--rival-page`. It only reprices or cancels its own targeted offers: team-desk counter-offers (`foreignOfferIds`, from the `deskOfferIds` registry) have the same shape and are not touched.
- **`rival-buy.ts`** — bids targeted at a rival team seen with a duplicate copy of a page card we are missing (`proposeRivalBuy`, `executeRivalBuy`, parameters in `RIVAL_BUY_PARAMS`). The gain is the loose surplus (`buyGain`): the page bonus only counts in `--page-targets` sets with `--page-bonus-scored`; and since they are all page cards, its budget stays above the page reserve (`RivalBuyInput.pageReserve`, that of `TradesRoute`). Structure only: copies seen with the team and confirmations from `/api/cards` (`GameState.rivals`); the most recent sighting must be ≤ 60 ticks old. Our ceiling = what the card contributes to us (`valueDelta`) minus margin 2, with no fee because the seller pays it on accepting (`maxBid` with `MAKER_FEES`); their estimated floor = median of per-set multipliers × book × marginal value of the second copy + 1. We bid floor + 30 % of the gap, capped, through `enforceGuardrails` and `fairPrice`; if the gap is < 2, no bid. Never for a card El Rastro is already bidding on or will bid on this tick (no double buy). Budget shared with El Rastro: `--max-spend` − spent − what El Rastro commits this tick, and cash − floor; at most 2 open, one per card. Offer with `to` at El Rastro (`give` cash, `want` the card), expires at 20 ticks; at 10 it is repriced toward the ceiling (at most 2 times) and then cancelled with a 40-tick wait. It is cancelled if we already have the card, the team no longer has the duplicate or the data is stale, El Rastro bids on it, the ceiling drops below the price or the agenda freezes new listings. Intents `markets:rivalbuy:post:<team>:<card>` (listing, lock `buy:<card>`) and `markets:rivalbuy:cancel:<offer>`. `[rival-buy] …` lines in the output of `pnpm bazaar:play`; live only with `--confirm` **and** `--rival-buy`. Priority (Pablo, 4 Oct, "negotiate all 3"): `priorityRefs` (MAL-04, MAL-06, MAL-09) go first; if one of them does not fit because of slots or budget, a non-priority bid is cancelled to make room for it. Budget: what the open bids commit is deducted in full before repricing; a raise that does not fit stays at its price, and if the total exceeds cash − floor, non-priority bids are cancelled first. We never bid targeted at the owner of `OFFER_VENUE` (t09 at v21: the server answers self_venue).
- **Epic lanes of `rival-buy.ts`** (`--rival-buy-epic`, together with `--rival-buy`; approved by Pablo on 4 Oct). `EPIC_BUY_LANES` carries two lanes, each with a ladder of 2 reprices (`epicStep`), expiry at 20 ticks and a cash floor of 100:
  - `EPIC_BUY_PARAMS` (test A): SAL-11, only to t18/t08/t17/t04 (t13 left on 4 Oct because it has no points room left). **A single bid at 185 = value − 49, no reprice** (decision of the coordinator and Pablo, 4 Oct): paying above value − min(50, room) does not score more. It only goes to a team whose room covers the whole gain (≥ min(50, value − price)). The ladder 185 → 200 → 215 went unfilled with t08, t17 and t04. A discard stores the rejected ceiling.
  - `EPIC_BUY_RET11`: RET-11, **open lane** (`open`): a single bid at El Rastro to anyone (no `to`) at 240, no reprice, lasting 40 ticks and reposted on expiry. It used to be targeted at t05/t12/t10, up to 240, and never filled. Targeted bids between teams fill ~3 % (26 of 811, 3 and 4 Oct). Coordinator OK with Pablo's rule, 4 Oct. El Rastro does not cancel it nor count it as its own spend (`foreignBidRefs` of `TradeState`, set by the coordinator with `--rival-buy-epic`).
  - The value of the epics is book × set multiplier (RET 1.6; SAL 1.3) and **carries no page bonus**: epics are not page cards. So the test measures whether a buy with a large gap scores the whole gap, not the page bonus.
  - Operation (`proposeEpicBuy`): a single open bid per card, and the figure goes through `enforceGuardrails` with reserve = ceiling. The cash floor of each lane also deducts what the others commit (an open bid does not hold cash on the server). If a team does not fill it at the ceiling, it is discarded (also in the same tick) and the next bid goes to the next holder. Discarded teams are stored in `results/bazaar-live/epic-done.json` (`defaultEpicDoneFile`, live only) and `seedEpicDone` loads them on startup, so a restart does not bid on them again.
  - When it does not bid: if we already have the card, if `/api/me/value` is not known or if it falls below the ceiling.
  - It sits outside `--max-spend`, with its own ceiling and floor. The bought card is not resold.
  - Goal: measure in `score-audit.jsonl` whether the page bonus scores.
- **`rival-swap.ts`** — targeted card-for-card swaps (`proposeRivalSwap`, `executeRivalSwap`, parameters in `RIVAL_SWAP_PARAMS`), opt-in with `--rival-swap` (without it nothing is proposed). Negotiation points come from the value gained against private value (LAT-04 at 4 P with value 1.8 gave +2.2), so we give a duplicate nobody buys for a card worth more. Structure only (`GameState.rivals`): the team needs X if they asked for it (`wants`, ≤ 60 ticks) or it is missing from a page within 2 of completion (`pages[].missing`) and they have not been seen with it; and they were seen with Y (`seen`, sighting or confirmation from `/api/cards` ≤ 60 ticks, like rival-buy). First the Ys of which they hold a duplicate (≥ 2 copies); with a single copy it is also requested (`allowSingleCopy`), behind those. Gain = value of Y − value of X − page risk − fee ≥ 2 (`swapGain`: `valueDelta` without the page bonus of a set that does not score, like `evaluateOffer`); fee 0 because we post (El Rastro charges whoever accepts, `MAKER_FEES`), but cash must cover El Rastro's fee (2 P) above `--cash-floor`. Never the last free copy (`isLastFreeCopy`), nor an asset that is locked, reserved, in another offer or that El Rastro or rival-page post this tick. Always at El Rastro (no fee for us and no market points given to another venue). At most 3 open, one per team and one per Y, one X per tick; expires at 20 ticks; after expiring or being cancelled, the same (X, team, Y) waits 40 ticks. It is cancelled if the asset gets occupied, the team stops needing X or stops having Y (or the data ages), the gain drops below 2 or the agenda freezes new listings. Intents `markets:rivalswap:post:<team>:<asset>` (listing; locks `asset:<id>`, `sell:<X>` and `buy:<Y>`, so no other sale of X nor purchase of Y leaves in the same tick) and `markets:rivalswap:cancel:<offer>`. Swaps targeted at us are evaluated by El Rastro (`evaluateOffer`, type swap: fee, margin 2, last free copy and page risk; one acceptance per tick, arbitrated with the locks of their assets); with `--rival-swap` they no longer require a card that closes a page. `[rival-swap] …` lines in the output of `pnpm bazaar:play`; live only with `--confirm` **and** `--rival-swap`.
