# src/broker/ — Broker

Broker of our venue (v04 "Team 2 · El Rastro Express", 0% and 0 P per card) with the `X-Broker-Key` header (`BAZAAR_BROKER_KEY` in .env.broker, ignored by git; never printed). The Market Test scores the gain between the hidden limits of matched bench offers: the price does not count, only which offers cross. **Warning**: with `mechanism: auto` the engine crosses the bench before the broker (stall level, half the points); the broker only beats the stall in a `board` venue. Entry: `main.ts` (`pnpm bazaar:broker`).

## Files

- **`broker.ts`** — pure. Tolerant `parseBrokerBook` (odd shapes go to `errors`, never throws); `benchRun` ("b3-7" → "b3"); `planBench`: per batch, ordered crossing (ascending asks against descending bids while bid ≥ ask, integer midpoint `fairPrice`, never outside [ask, bid]), each offer once; patience layer: `observeBench` tracks quotes between ticks, `temperOf` (new, relaxing, firm, settled), `estimatedLimit` (firm: `firmShade`; relaxing: one step more) sorts by estimated limit, `isUrgent` (relaxes now or age ≥ `maxAgeTicks`) and `holdTicks` defers new and patient pairs (0 by default = like the stall). `planPublic`: first `planExactAsset` (bid for a specific asset, `want.assets`, against the sale of that asset) and then `planAnyCopy` (bid for any copy, `want.cards` which the game stores as `want.types: ["card:REF"]`, against the sale of any series of that card); same card, another maker, bid ≥ ask + fee, integer midpoint, best surplus first, each offer and each asset once; at most `MAX_PUBLIC_MATCHES_PER_TICK` (10) per tick, those with the most surplus. `bookStateKey` avoids resending the same state.
- **`client.ts`** — `BrokerClient` (book, clock, markets, matches, announcement) with the `TokenBucket` from `../shared/client.ts`; `loadBrokerEnv`.
- **`agent.ts`** — `BrokerAgent.step`: reads clock and book, warns if the venue is auto, announces once, plans only if the state changed, does not reuse already matched offers, records rejections without stopping. `FileBrokerSink`: `results/bazaar-live/<date>/broker.jsonl` (tick, sell, buy, price, surplus, state), `bench.jsonl` (bench snapshots to calibrate patience) and `bench-raw.jsonl` (raw JSON of `/api/broker/book` during a bench, only when it changes, to see the real shape of the recent field). The bench in progress comes from the calendar with `ticksPerHourOf(tick, tHours, tickSeconds)`.
- **`shadow.ts`** — Market Test shadow (GET only, also in an auto venue): `BenchShadow` opens a session per bench (calendar or, without it, the whole hour) and, once per tick, `shadowStep` adds what auto crossed (`parseRecentBenchFills`, tolerant of several shapes of recent; real shape unverified: in session 1 it arrived empty or unrecognizable) and what `planBench` would have matched with the reconstructed book (what remains + the legs of each auto cross). Official truth on the auto side: `reconcileOfficial` (every 5 ticks, also outside the bench and on restart) reads `bench.finished` from the recorder's `results/bazaar-live/<date>/stream-team.jsonl` (`parseOfficialBench`, session → hour by `bench.started`) and stores it in `official` of the already recorded session; `updateRatio`: shadow/auto by surplus if auto was measured, otherwise shadow pairs / official `matches` (`ratioBasis: pairs`; shadow 0 ⇒ 0, the board was not shown to be better). Inferring crosses from offers that disappear does not work: in session 1 the ones that left together did not cross by quote (69 against 106, 65 against 95) and the official figure was 5. It persists in `results/bazaar-live/bench-sessions.json` (survives restarts); the process heartbeat goes to `results/bazaar-live/broker-heartbeat.json` (`saveHeartbeat`, `loadHeartbeat`). The coordinator reads it for `GameState.venue.mechanismDecision`.
- **`matchmaker.ts`** — matchmaker announcement (slide 9 of the «Payday» deck: «it finds the missing card»): `matchPairs` crosses each team's wishlists with the teams seen with a duplicate of that card (≥ 2 copies, ≤ 240 ticks) from the rivals book (`results/bazaar-live/rivals.json`, structure only) and leaves us out on both sides; `matchmakerAnnouncement` names them in the announcement of our current venue (≤ 1150 characters, the game stores 1200) or returns `announcementFor` if there are no pairs.
- **`main.ts`** — `runBrokerCli`: `pnpm bazaar:broker --dry-run --once` (GET only). Live requires removing `--dry-run` and passing `--confirm`; it runs endlessly. `--shadow` = `--dry-run --no-announce`; in dry-run it records the shadow, one `board shadow` line per tick (`boardShadowLine` from `shadow.ts`: exact and any-copy pairs and bench that a board would cross versus what auto would cross) and, in every mode, the heartbeat. Flags: `--poll-ms` (1000), `--hold-ticks` (0), `--firm-shade` (0.1), `--max-age-ticks` (6), `--max-public` (10), `--steps`, exits with code 3 after 3 consecutive reads rejected for a bad key (the venue key changed; the supervisor relaunches it with the one in .env.broker), `--no-announce`, `--announce-only` (posts the venue announcement once, `announcementFor` from `broker.ts` with the id and mechanism read from the book and from `/api/venues`, never a fixed id, and exits without matching; with `--dry-run` it only prints it; live it requires `--confirm` and Pablo's approval; with `--matchmaker` (and `--rivals-file`) the announcement names the pairs from `matchmaker.ts`). The announcement attracts organic flow (value created among other teams in v04, the other half of the market): the venues that receive the most (v07, v02) announce how to post with `venue`.
