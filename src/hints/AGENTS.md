# src/hints/ — Hints

Corpus of dealer lines per persona (`results/bazaar-live/hints.jsonl`), with candidates chosen by a deterministic rule. It never goes into a figure.

## Files

- **`corpus.ts`** — each dealer line per persona (tick, game time, wall time, thread, message, source our-thread or feed), from our threads and the feed (`collectRaw`, `newLines`); without `hints.jsonl`, `seedRaw` seeds it with everything saved in `results/` (dumps, scans, streams). It is deduplicated by message id or by persona + normalized text. `candidateReasons`, a deterministic rule with reasons: it names another persona or a stall, mentions an hour or "opens", words such as secret, rumour, hidden, legend, whisper, only the curious, a quoted phrase, an unpublished set or a card id outside the catalog. Each line also stores `targets`, the other personas it names (id, name or a word of the name: «Carmen» → abuela), because hints seed rumors about **other** stalls; an unnamed masculine pointer (`pointerTarget`, `POINTER_TARGETS`: in an Abuela line «ask him», «he will know», «pregúntale», «él sabrá» (ask him, he will know)) sends the line to the first known persona in the list: banco (Don Ernesto, the banker of level 5; the Moscow gold was the Banco de España's reserve) then picaros (the brothers Paco and Nando, each a "him") and otherwise `chato`, the only male stall next to hers (she names him in the same story: «El Chato next door knows more»; «Moscow gold» failed with him in English and in Spanish, threads 900 and 906), and it replaces the passing names (t02's egg, tick 505: «Ask him about the Moscow gold» was probed on Abuela again and never on Chato); it is narrow on purpose and only routes the probe, never a figure; and the probe keyword (`probeKeyword`: «about X» or «pregúntele … por X» (ask him … about X)). `enrichLine` redoes this deterministic part of the saved lines with today's rules and personas. It persists in `results/bazaar-live/hints.jsonl` (append only, across days; also in dry-run, because it only reads the game) and reaches each persona of the `GameState` (most recent first). A narrow exception to "only structure is read from the rival": **it never goes into a figure**.
- **`labels.ts`** — step 3, LLM labeling (opt-in, `pnpm bazaar:play --hint-llm`): `HintLabeler` sends unlabeled candidates in batches to `claude -p` (in the background, one call at a time of up to 240 s, because measured it takes about 2.5 min; it never blocks the tick; it shuts off after 3 failures). For each line, `classification` (hint, egg-clue or voice), `phrase` (the probe phrase, which `parseLabels` only accepts if it is a literal piece of the text and without digits) and `target` (the persona to ask). Cache in `results/bazaar-live/hint-labels.json`; `applyLabels` applies it in memory in the `GameState`.
- **`egg-plan.ts`** — the egg probe plan that Pablo approved for Sunday (`EGG_PLAN`): per dealer, the whole phrase, the literal substring the egg needs (`key`; an egg fires if the message contains it, ignoring accents and case), probability, what we put at stake and the cost. `eggPlanRows` computes the status (pending, sent, hit, miss, excluded) with our messages to personas from the team stream (`recordedOurPersonaMessages`) and our eggs, and the minimum tick of the next manual probe (≥ 5 ticks per dealer). It reaches `GameState.eggPlan` and the viewer ("Probe plan" table in Eggs). Never a figure.

The probe is chosen by `probePhrasesFor` (`../coordinator/routes.ts`): first hints from other stalls that point to this persona, then its egg-clue and finally its own keywords; `voice` lines never give a phrase.
