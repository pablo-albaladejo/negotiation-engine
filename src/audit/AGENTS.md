# src/audit/ — Inefficiency monitor (`pnpm bazaar:audit`)

Read-only: reads the day's local traces and warns about whatever costs us points (buying duplicates, selling below an open bid, losing the only copy of a page…). It never POSTs; at most a GET to `/api/me` on startup and every ≥ 5 min (`--no-api` avoids it).

## Files

- **`main.ts`** — CLI. `pnpm bazaar:audit --date YYYY-MM-DD`: processes the whole day, prints a report (alerts per detector, measured or not and why, gross P and P counting each overlapping loss only once) and exits. `pnpm bazaar:audit --watch`: reads what is new every `--interval` s (15) and appends only new alerts; a pass that fails, the first one included, is retried at the next interval (under `pnpm bazaar:up` it does not spend restarts). Outputs in `results/bazaar-live/<date>/` (`--out` changes the folder): `audit.jsonl` (one line per alert, deduplicated by `key` also across restarts; the last line of each `key` wins) and audit-status.json (overwritten: per detector, measured or not and why, alerts, P and last tick; per source, last tick and whether it is stalled). A source with more than 10 ticks without data while the clock advances leaves its detectors as not measured (unmeasured). **Retractions**: a line `{v:1, kind:"retract", key, detector, reason}` withdraws an alert already written. It is written when an alert changes severity or P (it retracts and writes the new version) and when a `key` of `dup-buy`, `album-copy-lost` or `repeated-price` stops appearing in a pass where the detector is measured; if it holds again, it is written again. The P totals (report and audit-status.json) come from the current evaluation, so they never count a withdrawn alert; audit-status.json lists the withdrawn `key`s in its retracted field.
- **`sources.ts`** — `Tail` (incremental reading by byte offset), tolerant parsers of the recorder stream (`stream-team.jsonl`/`stream-public.jsonl`; the team comes from its hello line), of `plan.jsonl` (with `PlanLineSchema` from [`src/coordinator/plan-log.ts`](../coordinator/AGENTS.md); a dealer line «· error ·» counts as a failure even if it comes with ok), of `decisions.jsonl` and of `play.log` (`PlayLogParser`: cash, cash floor, accept of selected markets, `post list`/`post bid` sent, dealer openings and failures) when there is no `plan.jsonl` for that tick. Also the duel counterattacks and acceptances that went out (`DuelSendParser`: only `[sent]` lines, with the agent's rule; from play.log and from the duel execution lines of `plan.jsonl`), all the dealer agent's decisions with their errors (`parseDealerEvent`) and the duel memory (duels-state.json: our prices without a tick). Baseline: the `/api/me` recorded in the day's dump and api-scan; catalog, the last one recorded; values, results/bazaar-live/values.json.
- **`value-history.ts`** — `ValueHistory`: our value of each card **at the deal's tick**, never today's (a page that advanced afterwards makes the card worth much more now: RET-02 was worth 16 P when sold at t230 and 122 P at the end of the day). Sources, best to worst: the `POST list|bid <ref> @ P P (…, value V)` lines of `plan.jsonl` and the `[trades] listing:` lines of play.log (sale: value = P − V; bid: value = P + V; maker fee 0), the `top sell edges` (value = bid − margin) and `top buy edges` (value = ask + margin) lines of play.log and, only if none of those is nearby, the reservation of a dealer thread in decisions.jsonl (a limit, not the value). The closest point within ±10 ticks is valid; one from a tick in which that card was also settled is discarded (it is not known whether its state already saw the deal). Since the API gives each copy the marginal of the last one, the value is normalized with the copies we held at that tick (`heldAt`, from the ledger's book).
- **`ledger.ts`** — `replay`: replays the stream in id order over the baseline. Per-card book (`Lot`: when, at what price and from where it came in and went out; packs and gifts included), our deals with the copies we held before (`OurTrade`, with the dealer thread and its reservation), each venue's order book at the moment of each deal (best bid and best ask from another team, with fee; without the offers directed at another team, nor the cancelled, expired or already matched ones) and our offer withdrawals.
- **`detectors.ts`** — `dup-buy` (loss = price − `nextCopyValue`, with the value at the purchase tick; it does not fire if the copy was resold at a profit within ≤ 30 ticks), `round-trip-loss` (sale − fee − purchase < 0 within ≤ 30 ticks; the same card or, failing that, another copy of the same reference), `buy-back` (selling and buying that reference back within ≤ 120 ticks paying more than was collected), `below-best-bid` / `above-best-ask` (≥ 1 P against the best open quote), `album-copy-lost` (last copy of a page card; high if it breaks a complete page; only if what was collected does not cover its value to us at the sale tick, `valueDelta`), `cash-floor` (cash below the floor), `reserve-breach` (commit 0ac4757: a purchase of a card that is not a page-reserve target leaves cash, the next tick's, below floor + reserve; as in the coordinator, only the reserve's references may touch it, and a purchase that completes another page also fires, with `completesPage` in the evidence. The per-tick reserve comes from the `page reserve: N P kept for <refs>` line of play.log, because `plan.jsonl` does not carry it; from the first line on, a tick without it kept nothing. Earlier purchases are a backtest with the first recorded reserve: low, because the reserve did not exist yet; with no line at all it stays unmeasured. It does not count P), `max-spend` (tick spend over `--max-spend`; without `plan.jsonl` it stays unmeasured), `double-act` (two routes buy or sell the same card in the same tick), `repeat-failure` (the same intent or `route:error` fails in ≥ 3 consecutive ticks), `churn` (the same offer posted and withdrawn ≥ 3 times, all less than 20 ticks apart: the El Rastro agent's slow repricing, every 10 ticks, does not count) and `stale-source`. `repeat-failure` does not count 429s nor retry codes (rate_limited, too_many_failures, wait_for_tick): day 2 hint 6, «a 429 means wait for the next tick». Only `dup-buy` and `album-copy-lost` value cards (`round-trip-loss`, `buy-back` and the book ones compare prices): both use `ValueHistory` and carry in the evidence the value's source and tick; if there is no historical value within ±10 ticks they use today's (source «current (hindsight)»), low severity (medium if it breaks a complete page), no `lossP` (the loss is reported separately, as a hindsight loss). Alerts that count a loss on the same deal (`evidence.trades`) carry `overlaps` with the keys of the others; the total without duplicates adds the largest of each group.

- **`conduct.ts`** — conduct detectors for the official day 2 hints (how we talk, not what we trade):
  - `repeated-price` (hint 5, «repeating a price is not a move»): the same figure in ≥ 2 consecutive offers of ours in a dealer thread (stream; `ourPrice` from decisions.jsonl if the stream does not have the thread) or in a duel (`[sent]`; duels-state.json if there are no lines). Low with 2, medium with ≥ 3; a deliberate hold (`final`, the rule) also fires, with the rule in the evidence. A duel stopped at our floor (limit + minSurplus selling, limit − minSurplus buying; the limit comes from the header `duel N · seller|buyer · price · limit L`) does not fire: there was no room to move (duel 2334: 115 with limit 114). Key: thread or duel + figure + start tick.
  - `duel-left-on-table`: a duel closed at a worse price than an offer the rival had already made and that we counter-offered instead of accepting (duel 2558: we were selling, the rival offered 136 at t560, we asked 139 and closed at 125). That offer was within the limit, because the deal was. P = the price difference, not counting the per-round decay. `repeated-price` is measured in halves: if decisions.jsonl is stalled, the duels stay measured.
  - `dealer-saturated`: a deal with a dealer whose level already has 3 deals of ours today, with no album reason (a sale, or a purchase of a card we already received today). Deals with dealers score only via the ladder (the top 3 per level, by the share of the range captured), never via neg_points, so with the level full one more almost never adds anything (Abuela selling commons at 5-6 P). Low severity and no P: the share of the range is not in the stream.
  - `hidden-card-move`: a hidden (or single-draw) card of ours announced, offered in a thread or settled. Hidden cards are not sold (Pablo, 3 Oct); the `isKeepsake` block prevents it, so an alert (high) means a route skipped it. It looks at announcements, offers with assets and settlements (which carry the ref); a thread opening only brings ids, so the attempt in thread 1495 (t1022, LAT-13 to the bank, no deal) does not show up.
  - `duel-unanswered` (hint 3, «a duel nobody answers gives 0 to both»): a duel of ours closed with no deal with the rival's last offer newer than ours (or with none of ours), or open with a rival offer unanswered for ≥ 3 ticks. If the silent one is the rival it does not fire. High from 11:30 (local reception time) and outside a practice session; low before. Without play.log or `plan.jsonl` it stays unmeasured.
  - `dealer-spam` (hint 5, «flood a dealer and it stops talking to you»): burst (≥ 2 openings + messages to the same dealer in one tick, above our design cadence), refusal (persona_quota, cooloff or judge warning, once per dealer, code and 60-tick episode) and retry (same dealer + target + code failing ≥ 3 times in 60 ticks). Threshold measured on 3 Oct: our cadence is ≤ 1 per dealer and tick (streaks of up to 25 consecutive ticks); a window of 18 in 20 fired 7 times and only 2 preceded a refusal, so it was discarded.

## Rules

- Reused valuation: `buildValueModel`, `valueDelta` and `tradeFee` from [`src/trades/`](../trades/AGENTS.md) and `nextCopyValue` from [`src/dealers/`](../dealers/AGENTS.md). Fees: El Rastro only charges whoever accepts (checked against our cash, ticks 320–380). A deal of ours carries a fee only if we accept; if an offer we posted was filled, 0. Other teams' quotes are valued as if we took them (net bid = bid − fee; ask = price + fee).
- Started by `pnpm bazaar:up` as the audit child (`--no-audit` removes it); see [`scripts/ops/`](../../scripts/ops/AGENTS.md).
