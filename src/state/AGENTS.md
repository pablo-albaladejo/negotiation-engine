# src/state/ — Game state

One `GameState` per tick, GET only and tolerant (whatever fails goes to `missing`). The coordinator and the viewer read it.

## Files

- **`game-state.ts`** — `buildGameState`: one `GameState` per tick, GET only, in parallel and tolerant (every read that fails goes to `missing`). Ours: cash, level, album by page, cards and duplicates, known private values (`your_value`, local use only), breakdown of the figure (`extractScoreFields`), venue, open threads, cooloff per dealer, strikes if `/api/me` returns them (not today). Environment: clock, `readLimits` (`clock.limits`), the tick's feed (`events`, 200 events: the coordinator's score audit reads our settlements from there), calendar, dealers, live duels, El Rastro book, our offers, leaderboard (every `--leaderboard-every` ticks). `formatGameState` gives the summary without private values. `news` (`NewsSignals`, from [`src/news/`](../news/AGENTS.md)): the news as a hint, read from today's news-summary.json (option `newsDir`; with no file or a broken one, empty) and without calling the API; they may be rumor, never a figure, and no decision reads them yet (coordinator, dealers, duels, El Rastro, broker and engine do not touch `GameState.news`). One «news» line in the output. `goals` (from [`src/goals/`](../goals/AGENTS.md), `readGoalsState`): team goals and the registry of strategies and proposals read from `results/state/`; display only, no route decides with them and the figures of a proposal are text for humans. `workshop` (from [`src/workshop/`](../workshop/AGENTS.md)): duplicates by rarity with the guardrails of a sale, expected value against cost and the decision (craft, hold, short). `venue.mechanismDecision` (auto or board? see [`src/venue/`](../venue/AGENTS.md)) is filled in by the coordinator. Each tick also writes `results/bazaar-live/<date>/venue-books.json` (list of venues and the book of each one, the same GETs as the price sheet; atomic write that does not stop the tick if it fails) for the viewer's «Venues» tab, with no extra GETs.
- **`conversation.ts`** — `Conversation`, a first-class entity: one per thread with a dealer, live duel and our offer in El Rastro (`buildConversations`). Identity, goal (ladder, page, duplicate, duel...) and expected value, private limits (reserve, private value, `your_limit`, `your_days_weight`; never in a message), structural-only history (prices and current offer, never their text), phase (opening, haggling, closing, done: messages used against estimated patience, final offer, deadline), its concession, mood (warnings, strikes, cooloff, friendliness), hints and eggs (markers for step 3), turn (filled in by the coordinator) and outcome. `strategy`: plan by code (`dealerStrategy` with the negotiator's `plannedSchedule` and the dealer's profile; `duelStrategy` with the curve, the acceptance threshold and the day plan), last decision (action, figure, rule and fixed code reason) and what happens if the other side does not move. What the API does not give (forced phase, mood, hints, eggs, last decision) persists in `results/bazaar-live/<date>/conversations.json` (`saveConversationMemos`, live only).
- **`world.ts`** — personas, eggs, gifts and flags. `buildPersonas`: personas are NOT a fixed list (no hand-written id): they come from `/api/dealers`, `/api/levels` and the feed (`level.announced`, `level.activated`, `level.unlocked`), with type (dealer, collector, trickster, banker; dealer by default), state (announced, active, unlocked-for-us, closed), unlock (early_deals_with, early_min_deals, open_to_all_at), our progress (`negotiatedDeals`: deals with the previous dealer at a price different from its opening), prize if the API exposes it, traits (a new persona takes its parameters from them with `negotiatorForDealer`), egg hints and probes. Trickster: the flag detector looks from the first message. `worldFromFeed`: eggs found by others and the ones left per persona (**ASSUMPTION** `EGGS_PER_PERSONA_ASSUMPTION` = 15 while the API does not give max_total), and what is ours: eggs, badges, hidden cards, gifts (gift.given) and flags with their outcome and balance. The feed (`/api/feed`, 200 events) loses eggs within minutes: `buildGameState` adds to it the day's egg.found, egg.given, badge.awarded and gift.given that the recorder keeps (`recordedWorldEvents`: stream-public.jsonl and stream-team.jsonl in the day's folder) without duplicating by id (`mergeWorldEvents`), and the badges from `/api/me` take precedence for ours. This way egg probes resolve as a hit even if the event is no longer in the feed. These persist: `results/bazaar-live/<date>/personas.json` (hints and probes) and `results/bazaar-live/<date>/flags.json` (flags sent), live only. `Conversation` gains `patience` (probeCostNow 0 at opening or after a deal, 1 otherwise) and flagCandidate. Each persona carries *estimates* and each dealer conversation *prediction* (per-persona fit, see [`src/dealers/history/`](../dealers/history/AGENTS.md)); `buildGameState` fills them in with `updatePosterior`.
- **`persona-model.ts`** — `PersonaModel` (`Persona.model`, exposed by `/api/bazaar/model` in `personas[].model`): the accumulated model of each persona, one field per YAML parameter (personas.md §1 and §3) with `{value, lo, hi, n, source: measured|prior|public|unknown, lastTick}`. Public and exact: kind, level, traits, deals_per_team_per_hour, unlock and menu (bands, sets, list_price, per_team_per_hour). Inferred: `strategy` (opening_markup for selling and buying, β, max_rounds, walk_after_rounds, patience_jitter, accept_margin, mirror_concessions, welcome_first_deal, welcome_price_frac per side, limit_jitter, demand_markup, politeness_discount; the last two unknown), `bands` (measured floor_frac or ceiling_frac and the limit in P), `trades` (stock_per_hour and budget_per_hour, unknown until a «refuse» is seen), `anti_cheat` (strikes and cooloffs seen), `trickster` (traps and swapped cards seen through correct flags) and `fired` (hints and eggs). templates, model, prompt_base, voice and knowledge are not observed and are not there (`UNOBSERVABLE`). `buildGameState` builds it each tick from the per-persona fit (which WRITES it, see [`src/dealers/history/`](../dealers/history/AGENTS.md)) and accumulates it on top of the previous tick's (`lastTick` only changes if the value changes); the coordinator (mirror, arbitrage) and the viewer READ it. `Persona.estimates` remains for the viewer. Persists in results/bazaar-live/persona-model.json (`loadPersonaModels`, `savePersonaModels`; with `bazaar:play`, also in dry-run, GET only); if it does not exist, the first tick migrates from results/bazaar-live/persona-posterior.json (the estimates are rebuilt from the observations). `formatPersonaModel`: one line per persona in the play output. Structure only, never a figure from a text.
- **`valuation.ts`** — `buildValuation` builds `GameState.valuation` once per tick: the value model (`buildValueModel` from `src/trades/trades.ts`) made visible. Per card: the API value we know, the base (first copy, no bonus) and whether it comes from the API or is estimated (set multiplier × book), what one more copy adds (`nextCopy`) and what losing one costs (`loseCopy`, with the page risk from `marginalValue` in `src/markets/scanner.ts`). Per page: the bonus at stake. Also, the rules (marginals per copy, page and set bonus) and the value of the portfolio. Display only: today the routes still rebuild their model; the price sheet takes `nextValue` from here. Local use: never goes in a message.
- **`prices.ts`** — `buildPriceSheet` (and `buildVenues`, the venue table, with the deals housed in each one in `trades`), the price sheet (`GameState.markets.prices`): one entry per card of the published sets (a new set enters when it becomes `released`) with book, rarity, print run, minted and scarcity, hidden, dealers that sell or buy it (from the menu), best ask and bid in El Rastro and in the other open venues (venue and offer, excluding ours), last single-card deal in the feed (settlement; if none, unknown), our private value (from `/api/me` and from the cache `results/bazaar-live/values.json`, with at most 4 value GETs per tick via `valuesWanted`), how many we have, what one more copy adds (`nextValue`: if we already have it, only its marginal with the value model from `src/trades/trades.ts`, because the `your_value` of the copy we have carries the page bonus we already collected; measured 4 Oct: RET-03 is worth 122 but a second copy 4) and the gaps: buyEdge = nextValue − ask, sellEdge = bid − value, and whether it completes a page (neg_points adds the value gained to private value, with no cap: [neg-points-formula](../../docs/bazaar/neg-points-formula.md)). `formatPriceSheet` prints the 5 best buy and sell gaps.
- **`rivals.ts`** — what the other teams have and what they look for, using public structure only (never text). A card is **seen** with a team if it receives it in a settlement, offers it (`give.assets` carries the asset id, in El Rastro, another venue or a dealer thread) or it comes out as the best card of a pack (pack.opened event); it is **sought** if the team asks for it (`want.assets`, `want.types` `card:REF`) or opens a thread to buy it. The leaderboard bounds each collection (`album_filled`, `pages_complete`, `rarest`). `RivalLedger` accumulates across ticks in `results/bazaar-live/rivals.json` (`loadRivalLedger`, `saveRivalLedger`; the first time it is seeded with the recorder's `stream-public.jsonl` because the feed is short); `buildGameState` updates it in place and fills `GameState.rivals` (`rivalsView`: per team, cards seen, duplicates, pages with what they lack, `unseen` = `album_filled` − page cards seen, and what they ask for and have not yet obtained; `byRef`: who has and who seeks each card). It also keeps each leaderboard row per team (`history`, at most `MAX_HISTORY`) to see their evolution. With `rivalConfirmPerTick` (the coordinator passes 2; the viewer, 0) it requests `/api/cards/{id}` for the page cards we lack that a rival has, the longest-ago confirmed first (`confirmCandidates`, fresh `CONFIRM_FRESH_TICKS`): no movement since we saw it, `confirmedTick`; if it moved, it passes to its new owner or, if the API hides it («a team»), it leaves the rival's list (`applyCardHistory`). If it fails, it goes to `missing` and the tick continues. `formatRivals`: the teams closest to completing a page. `tradeSignals` summarizes the view for El Rastro (`TradeState.rivals`): demand = cards asked for in the last 60 ticks or that leave a team ≤ 2 from a page; supply = cards seen or confirmed with a rival in the last 60 ticks.
- **`time.ts`** — `buildTime`: tick, game hour, wall-clock hour, phase of the day (open or closed), round and its weight (`leaderboard.rounds`; if not read, the previous tick's), seconds per tick, ticks left today, drift against the plan (one wall-clock hour with doors open is one game hour; the plan comes from `clock.days` and the calendar's `day_opens`) and `scheduleChanged` if the calendar's fingerprint changed since the previous tick («re-read»). `formatTime`: «h 2.65 · R1 ×0.5 · closed until Sat 09:00 · …».
