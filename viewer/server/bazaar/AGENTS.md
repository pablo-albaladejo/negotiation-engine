# viewer/server/bazaar/ — `/api/bazaar/*` endpoints

What the viewer serves on top of the Bazaar API and `results/bazaar-live/`. Towards the Bazaar, GET only; never the key in a response.

## Files

- **`bazaar.ts`** — `bazaarScore` reads the `score.jsonl` files from `VIEWER_BAZAAR_DIR` (default `results/bazaar-live`), read-only and traversal-proof. `BazaarLive.get` (`GET /api/bazaar/live`) only calls the live Bazaar (`GET /api/me` + `/api/clock`) when `BAZAAR_KEY` is in the server's own environment (loaded like `src/shared/env.ts`); it returns only the public fields of the figure and the clock, never the key, cached ~5 s. `BazaarThreads.get` (`GET /api/bazaar/threads`) calls `GET /api/me/threads` + `GET /api/threads/{id}` (the 6 most recent/open) and adds our local trace (`thread-<id>.jsonl` under `VIEWER_BAZAAR_DIR`) if it exists; `BazaarDuels.get` (`GET /api/bazaar/duels`) calls `GET /api/duels` (open and, if the server supports it, `?done=true`). Both cached ~5 s, only with `BAZAAR_KEY`, never the key in the response. **Unified `#bazaar` tab** with a real-time view of the board (threads, duels, trades, offers between teams).
- **`bazaar-board.ts`** + **`bazaar-board-core.ts`** — **`GET /api/bazaar/board`**: unified view of the Bazaar in the `#bazaar` tab. One cycle per tick (scheduled with `next_tick_in` from `/api/clock`, shared cache and a single flight) does GETs only (`/api/clock`, `/api/leaderboard`, `/api/feed`, `/api/venues/rastro/offers` without key; `/api/me`, `/api/me/threads`, `/api/duels` (+`?done=true`), `/api/me/offers`, `/api/venues/<ours>/offers` and `/api/me/value` with key) through a bucket of ≤ 2 req/s. The pure core builds ONE list (threads with dealers, duels, deals and offers between teams) with price, value of what was received/delivered, surplus, verdict (≥ 1 good, ≤ −1 bad), the Δ from `score.jsonl` by `cause.thread`, ticks, literal messages, offers and our `decisions.jsonl`. The value is computed once and stored in `<VIEWER_BAZAAR_DIR>/<date>/verdicts.json` (the only file the viewer writes), along with all the feed's settlements, ours and other teams' (the feed is short, so they are also read from the recorder's `stream-public.jsonl`). Other teams' settlements come out separately in `others` (`otherTradeRows`): public structure only, no value or verdict. The books (El Rastro and our venue) bring the 40 most recent offers and always ours. For the cockpit it adds `/api/catalog` (hourly), `/api/schedule` and `/api/me/value` for ≤ 3 missing cards per cycle (remembered until our cards change). Optional read-only fallback: `VIEWER_BAZAAR_SNAPSHOTS` (by default the causa-prima monitor's snapshots file). Never the key in the response. Points per deal: `applyScoreAudit` joins score-audit.jsonl to each deal by settlement; a ladder-only Δ with no deal goes to the last dealer deal within the previous `LADDER_LAG` (6) ticks, marked `d_lagged`. Duel points: `applyDuelPoints` joins `duel-points.jsonl` (written by the duels session since Duels III: the duel_points jump at the first tick we saw it and the duels that closed) to each duel; with several duels in the same tick the jump belongs to all of them (`d_shared`), and `backfill` is marked `d_backfill`. The practice (session 1) and Duels I and II (sessions 2 and 3) have no file and stay «not audited»; session 4 is Duels III and session 5, the Grand Final (`duelSessionName`).
- **`bazaar-model.ts`** — **`GET /api/bazaar/model`**: our internal model. Once per tick (cache and a single flight, ≥ 30 s) it imports the functions from `src/`: `buildGameState`, the coordinator routes (`DuelsRoute`, `DealersRoute`, `TradesRoute`) in dry-run with `propose` only, and `arbitrate`/`budgetFrom`; it completes each conversation with turn, strategy and last decision like `bazaar:play`. **It never calls the routes' execute method.** Two-layer guard: `ReadOnlyBazaarClient` (its `raw` rejects everything that is not GET/HEAD with `ModelPostBlocked`) and `readOnlyFetch` (wraps fetch); attempts are counted in `safety.blocked`. ≤ 1.5 own req/s; it reuses the board's feed, catalog, El Rastro and `/api/me` (`recentFeed`, `marketInputs`). It reads the latest date's conversations.json, personas.json and flags.json from `results/bazaar-live/<date>/`, and the tail of `hints.jsonl`; it adds `/api/schedule` and `/api/venues`. It reads (never saves) `results/bazaar-live/persona-posterior.json` and passes it to `buildGameState`: each conversation with a dealer carries *prediction* and each persona *estimates*; the `fit` field repeats the per-persona estimates (dealer side only) and lists in `welcome` the conversations marked as first with that dealer. It computes `state.venue.mechanismDecision` with `decideMechanism` and the shadow's sessions (read-only). It returns the `GameState`, budget, `ACCEPT_PRIORITY`, intents per route with verdict, goals, agenda, triggers, eggs from the feed, prices (`state.markets.prices` or reconstructed), packs, venues and `news` (`GameState.news`, from today's news-summary.json; a hint, never a figure). It carries private data: 127.0.0.1 only. The rivals book (results/bazaar-live/rivals.json from play or, if missing, the recorder) is read once and updated in memory; the viewer does not save it. It carries `ladder`: the day's dealer ladder by level (`ladderLevels` over lessons.json and the persona model, same as `DealersRoute`).
- **`bazaar-now.ts`** — pure core of the `now` field of `/api/bazaar/model` («Now» tab): the goal of each intent (`goal.why` and global goal) and its code-decided figure, deadlines (duels and offers), our open offers in any venue (price, age, commission, whether they cross another's offer in the same book) and our venue (status, opening, pending commission). For that the model adds GETs only: `/api/me/offers`, `/api/duels` and the book of at most 3 venues where we have offers. If a build is already running, `GET /api/bazaar/model` returns it right away with `rebuilding: true` and builds the next one in the background (a single flight).
- **`bazaar-cockpit-core.ts`** — pure core of the cockpit: copies we hold (`holdingsOf`), album pages with the missing cards and, for the sticker grid, all the page's cards (`cards`: copies, value, book, roll and minted), the shinies (`shinies`; hidden ones only if we have them) and the set's color and theme (`albumOf`) and future calendar (`scheduleOf`). Also `scoreNumbers`/`scorePartsOf`: `board.score_parts` (each part of `/api/me` → score now, at the day's baseline and at the previous tick; the baseline is the first snapshot after the daily reset —the last tick in which neg_points, duel_points and ladder_points drop to 0 at once, `dailyResetIndex`, marked `day_reset`— or, if there was none, the day's first snapshot; and `series`: each part from that baseline, at most 80 points, for each value's «Today» line; and `week_series`: the same over all recorded days, the earlier ones read once a day, for the «Week» line); `bazaar-board.ts` stores one snapshot per tick in `results/bazaar-live/<date>/score-parts.jsonl` (written by the viewer, like verdicts.json). And `teamDeskOf`: `board.team_desk` from `results/bazaar-live/<date>/team-desk.jsonl` (written by `bazaar:play`): per-team chains incoming offer → counteroffer → reductions → result (key: id of the incoming offer; otherwise the offer it replaces or the same card), with neg earned (Σ `negDelta` of the filled ones) and pending (`negIfFilled` of the last counteroffer of the pending chains: counteroffer sent —not a dry-run «would»—, with no result and not expired; the expiry of the incoming offer and of ours comes from the public stream's «offer.listed», `offerExpiriesOf`). On the card, «would» is shown as «not sent (dry-run)» and, if the incoming offer already expired, «expired, not sent».
- **`bazaar-workshop.ts`** — `workshopOf`: El Taller (`POST /api/taller`, 3 duplicates of one rarity → 1 random card of the next, unscored) in `board.workshop`. Free duplicates per rarity with the sale guardrails (never a hidden card or other `isKeepsake`; `freeCounts` from `src/shared/last-copy.ts`, `assetsInThreads`/`assetsInOffers` from `src/shared/asset-locks.ts`: never the last free copy nor anything in an open thread or offer), opening (`level.activated` with `how`) and «taller.crafted» from the public stream (deduplicated by id). It never sends.
- **`bazaar-news.ts`** — **`GET /api/bazaar/news`**: only reads news-summary.json (written by `pnpm bazaar:news`, [`src/news/`](../../../src/news/AGENTS.md)) for today's local date in `VIEWER_BAZAAR_DIR` or, if there is none yet, for the most recent one that has it; it never calls the Bazaar. Without a file: `{ available: false }`.
- **`bazaar-agents.ts`** — status of dealers, duels and broker by the hour and the last line of their trace in `results/bazaar-live/<date>/` (read-only).
